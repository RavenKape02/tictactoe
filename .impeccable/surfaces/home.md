# Surface: / (the game)

Mode: Experience. The visitor is inside the work: the board is the page.
Audience and job: CAPM / Semprove reviewers checking three things within seconds (turns alternate, winner or draw shown, New Game restarts), on desktop or phone.
Scene: a reviewer at a desk in daytime office light, between other candidates. Light scene, warm daylight on wood.
Constraints: public Vercel URL; WebGL fallback; reduced motion; keyboard and screen reader play; scores persist with a separate Reset scores.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tic-Tac-Toe as a real wooden toy on a desk, played by touching the board itself. Refuses the category default of a flat 3x3 grid of buttons in a centered card.

OWN-WORLD: Natural beech board with carved dark grooves on a walnut desk. Pieces are lacquered toy primaries: cobalt-blue X blocks, vermilion O rings, glossy. A canary-yellow legal-pad sheet (blue rules, red margin) holds the score as ballpoint tally strokes. Controls are beech blocks with an edge shadow. Type: Bricolage Grotesque for display and status, tabular numerals.

STORY: The visitor sees whose turn it is, places a piece by touching a square (a ghost piece previews the move), watches it drop and settle, sees the winning three hop and spin or a draw called, and presses New Game. The tally keeps count.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D desk. Beech board centered, about 60% of viewport height, camera about 50 degrees down. Top left: a large status line ("Blue's move", "Red wins", "Draw"). Right on desktop, below on phone: the tilted legal pad with X, O and Draws tallies, then a beech New Game block. Small credit line at the bottom left.

FORM: Pinned by the user ("Tabletop"). seed: none (pinned) "a warm 3D wooden board on a desk, with chunky X and O pieces that drop in and settle, soft shadows, and a paper-style scoreboard".
Signature interaction: ghost-piece preview on hover or focus, then a drop with a damped settle. Motion grammar: drop, settle, a winning hop; nothing else moves on its own.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
